Privacy Policy

Effective September 19, 2026 · Applies to Paizal version 1.0.2
Summary: Paizal version 1.0.2 is designed to work locally on your device. Paizal does not collect, transmit, sell, or use your app data for advertising or tracking.

Information stored on your device

Paizal can store information you enter to create and manage checks, including bank account details, routing and account numbers, check records, recipients, templates, check settings, and your local security configuration. This information is stored locally by the app and is protected using on-device security mechanisms, including an encrypted local database.

No account required for core use

Paizal version 1.0.2 does not require you to create an online account to use its core check-writing and management features. Optional online Account Recovery is disabled in this production version.

Data collection and tracking

For the current production version, Paizal does not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, crash-reporting SDKs, or tracking technologies. Paizal does not sell personal information and does not share app data with advertisers.

Banking and payments

Paizal is a check-writing and check-management tool. It does not connect to your bank, move money, initiate ACH or wire transfers, deposit checks, or provide banking or financial advisory services.

Biometric security

If you enable Face ID, authentication is handled by Apple's operating system. Paizal does not receive or store your biometric data.

Future online recovery features

If optional online recovery features are enabled in a future production version, this Privacy Policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be reviewed and updated before that functionality is offered.

Children

Paizal is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Paizal evolves. The effective date shown above will be updated when material changes are made.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, email paizal.support@gmail.com or visit the Paizal Support page.
