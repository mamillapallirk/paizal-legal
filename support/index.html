Paizal Support

Pay Smart. Pay Safe.
Paizal helps you create, preview, print, organize, and track checks while keeping your check-writing data protected on your device.

Getting Help

For help with Paizal, email paizal.support@gmail.com. Please describe what you were trying to do, what happened, and your Paizal app version if known. Do not email bank account numbers, routing numbers, PINs, or other sensitive financial information.

Check Printing

Paizal is designed for printing onto compatible preprinted check stock. If printed text is not aligned correctly, use Paizal's Alignment settings and test carefully before printing financial documents.

Security & Privacy

Core Paizal data is stored locally on your device. You can protect access with a PIN and Face ID where supported. For details, read the Paizal Privacy Policy.

Feedback

Feature requests and product feedback are welcome at paizal.support@gmail.com.

Important

Paizal is a check-writing and management tool. It does not connect to your bank, move money, deposit checks, or initiate ACH or wire transfers.
